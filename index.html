Logistics
Festa dell'amore will start on Friday 11th September at 5pm and it will go on until Saturday evening. On Friday, we will have a little ceremony, aperitivo, and dinner. On Saturday, a chill morning and then some activities during the day and in the evening.
How to get there
If you fly, Rome Fiumicino airport is probably your best choice. The closes station to Torrita is Poggio Mirteto Scalo and there is a direct train from Fiumicino (1h 40 min, quite regularly). From Poggio Mirteto we can pick you up by car. Alternatively, from Ciampino airport it is also possible to get a bus and then a train, but just a bit more complex. If you want to rent a car it's also an option.
Accommodation
Sleeping spots are unfortunately limited but we will make sure to give a bed to anyone that does not have a place to stay in Rome for Friday and Saturday night (someone will have to stay in a tent ⛺️). Lavinia and I will be there already some days before and will remain some days after, let us know your plans and we can organize something!
