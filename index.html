Torrita Tiberina, Rome, Italy

Lavinia's grandfather left the chaos of Rome to find refuge in this little town. The place has a beautiful view over the Tiber river and it is where Lavi's mum got married.

The house is well connected to Rome via train (station Poggio Mirteto Scalo) from there it will be easy to pick you up with a car ❤
