Logistics
Festa dell'amore will start on Friday 11th September at 5pm and it will go on until Saturday evening. On Friday we will have the main ceremony, aperitivo, and dinner. This is the main event, so if you can only stay for one day don't miss this! On Saturday, we will have a chill morning and then some activities during the day and in the evening.
How to get there
If you fly, Rome Fiumicino airport is probably your best choice. There is a direct train to Poggio Mirteto Scalo (1h 40 min, quite regularly). From there we can pick you up by car. Alternatively, from Ciampino airport it is also possible to get a bus and then a train, but just a bit more complex. If you want to rent a car that is also an option but not really necessary.
Accommodation
Sleeping spots are unfortunately limited but we will make sure to give a bed to anyone that does not have a place to stay in Rome for Friday and Saturday night (someone will have to stay in a tent ⛺️). We will be there already some days before and will remain some days after, so you can arrive before Friday or stay until Sunday if more convenient, just write us and we can organize!
